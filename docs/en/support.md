# Support — LUNIA

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

## Contact us

Write to **hello@lunia.health**. We answer on working days, usually within 2 days.

In the app you can also send feedback, report a bug or leave a suggestion under **Profile**.

## In an emergency

LUNIA is not an emergency service and does not replace your doctor. If you are in danger, call **112**. For health advice in Portugal, call **SNS 24: 808 24 24 24**.

## Subscriptions

To change plan or cancel, in the app go to **Profile → Account → Manage your plan → Manage subscription**. You can also do it in the store:

- **iPhone:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LUNIA.
- **Android:** Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → LUNIA.

Your subscription renews automatically until you cancel it. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. If you changed phone, use **Restore purchases** on the plans screen.

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google in Google Play.

## Delete your account

In the app, under **Profile → Account → Delete account**. You can export your data first with **Export my data**, on the same screen. More in [Account Deletion](account-deletion.md).

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription: cancel it first, as above.
