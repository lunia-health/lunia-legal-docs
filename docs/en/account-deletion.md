# Account Deletion — LUNIA

_Last updated: April 16, 2026_

LUNIA is developed by Virtuous Conviction Lda. (operating as LUNIA Health).

## How to delete your account

You may delete your account at any time via Profile → Delete Account or by contacting hello@lunia.health. Upon deletion: all local data is immediately cleared from your device, server-side data deletion cascades within 72 hours, forum posts are anonymised, consent audit logs are retained as required by law. Before deleting, you may export your data via Profile → Export My Data.

**Deletion is permanent. Once the data is erased it cannot be recovered, by you or by us, so export anything you want to keep first.**

## What is kept, and for how long

- Account data: until account deletion.
- Onboarding conversations: 30 days after completion.
- Symptom diary entries: until account deletion or user request.
- Chat history: until account deletion.
- Wearable health data: cached locally; server sync until deletion.
- Tempo behavioral data: until consent withdrawal or account deletion.
- Clinical reports: until account deletion; PDF links expire after 48h.
- Forum posts: until user deletion or moderation removal.
- Consent audit logs: retained indefinitely (GDPR regulatory requirement).
- Device identifier hashes (abuse prevention): 12 months after the device was last seen, then deleted automatically.

Write to hello@lunia.health and we will handle the request.
