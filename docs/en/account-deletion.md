# Account Deletion — LUNIA

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

LUNIA is developed by Virtuous Conviction Lda. (operating as LUNIA Health).

## How to delete your account

You may delete your account at any time via Profile → Account → Data & privacy → Delete account or by contacting hello@lunia.health. Upon deletion: your account is erased at once and you are signed out on every device; all local data is cleared from your device at the same time; your health data, your conversations with Luni and your notifications are erased from our servers, your data at RevenueCat is deleted and any subscription bought on the web is cancelled, normally within minutes — if one of our systems is unavailable we keep retrying until it confirms; if you signed in with Apple, we ask Apple to revoke LUNIA's access to your Apple ID; the text and authorship of your forum posts are erased; consent and terms-acceptance records are kept for 3 years after the account ends, to prove consent and to establish or defend legal claims, and then deleted; the payment records Stripe holds for your invoices are kept for 10 years, as tax law requires. Copies in our encrypted backups are deleted automatically within 31 days. A subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play is not cancelled when you delete your account: cancel it in the store's subscription settings before deleting your account, so you are not charged again. Before deleting, you may export your data via Profile → Account → Data & privacy → Export my data.

**Deletion is permanent. Once the data is erased it cannot be recovered, by you or by us, so export anything you want to keep first.**

## Deleting some data without closing your account

You do not have to delete your account to remove data. In the app, under My space > Calendar, you can delete individual diary entries, and in the conversation history you can delete a conversation. Deleted entries are removed from our servers as well. You can also write to hello@lunia.health and ask us to remove specific data.

## What is kept, and for how long

- Account data: until account deletion.
- Onboarding conversations: 30 days after completion.
- Symptom diary entries: until account deletion or user request.
- Chat history: until account deletion.
- Wearable health data: until account deletion or consent withdrawal.
- Tempo behavioral data: until consent withdrawal or account deletion.
- Clinical reports: until account deletion; the link emailed to a professional expires after 48 hours.
- Data exports (files to download in the app): 72 hours, then deleted automatically.
- Forum posts: until user deletion or moderation removal; when you delete your account, their text and authorship are erased.
- Consent and terms-acceptance records (including the IP address you gave them from): kept for 3 years after the account ends, to prove consent and to establish or defend legal claims, and then deleted.
- Crisis alerts: 2 years after they are resolved, and deleted earlier if you delete your account.
- Device identifier hashes (abuse prevention): 12 months after the device was last seen, then deleted automatically.
- Operational logs (service activity, errors and a pseudonymous account ID; never health data or message content), kept under our legitimate interest in running and securing the service: 25 days, then deleted automatically.
- Diagnostic traces of each chat turn (conversation ID, detected intent, model used, token counts, tools used, the safety check outcome and emergency type; never message content): 30 days, removed by the following weekly cleanup, so up to 37 days, and deleted earlier when you clear your chats or delete your account.
- Payment records at Stripe (customer, invoices and payments, no health data): 10 years, as Portuguese tax law requires (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) and Art. 17(3)(b)), even after you delete your account.
- Backups (encrypted copies of our database, kept only for disaster recovery): up to 31 days, then deleted automatically, including data you have already deleted.
